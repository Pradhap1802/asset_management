✨ Features

Asset Types & Classification

Categorize assets into types with built-in depreciation rules, methods, and frequencies per category.

Asset Transfer Management

Track asset assignments to employees with transfer codes, assign/return dates, and full audit trail.

Maintenance & Repair

Log maintenance records, track vendor costs, downtime days, and manage repair status with document attachments.

Depreciation Tracking

Auto-calculate depreciation using percentage or fixed methods on yearly/monthly/daily frequencies.

Asset Label Printing

Print professional asset labels in multiple formats (2x7, 4x7, 4x12, Dymo) with barcodes and asset info.

Reporting & Analytics

Visual dashboards with asset value, depreciation, maintenance cost and transfer history reports.

🔄 Operational Workflows

Comprehensive end-to-end asset lifecycle management

1. Seamless Accounting Integration

Streamline your procurement process. Assets can be automatically created directly from Accounting Vendor Bills. This ensures that every capital expenditure is captured and tracked from the moment of purchase, eliminating manual data entry errors.

  • Bill-to-Asset Conversion
  • Linked Financial Documents
  • Accurate Cost Basis Tracking

2. Intuitive Dashboard

Get a 360-degree view of your entire asset portfolio. Our dynamic dashboard provides real-time insights into total asset value, maintenance costs, upcoming warranty expirations, and depreciation trends.

  • Real-time Financial Metrics
  • Maintenance Performance KPIs
  • Visual Trend Analysis

3. Category-based Analytics

Analyze your assets through granular categorization. Our specialized dashboard allows you to filter and view metrics based on Asset Categories. Compare performance, maintenance costs, and depreciation across different equipment types like IT, Machinery, or Furniture.

  • Category Performance View
  • Cross-category Cost Analysis
  • Dynamic Category Filters

4. Centralized Asset Registry

Manage the entire journey of your assets from procurement to retirement. Register assets directly from Purchase Invoices or manually. Assign serial numbers, track current locations, and manage technical specifications in one place.

  • Automated Asset Onboarding
  • Multi-branch Asset Tracking
  • Integrated Barcode Labels

5. Smart Warranty Tracking

Never miss a warranty claim again. Track warranty periods, service agreements, and insurance details for every asset. The system sends automatic alerts before expiration to help you optimize repair costs.

  • Proactive Expiration Alerts
  • Document Storage (Invoices/Certificates)
  • Insurance Renewal Reminders

6. Inter-branch Transfers

Effortlessly move assets between locations or departments. Our transfer workflow maintains a complete custody chain, including transit tracking and receiver acknowledgment for total transparency.

  • Multi-location Support
  • Custody Chain History
  • Transit Status Monitoring

7. Automated Depreciation

Automate your financial accounting with precision. Support for multiple depreciation methods (Straight-line, Declining Balance) with automatic journal entry generation and real-time book value updates.

  • Automatic Accounting Sync
  • Multiple Calculation Strategies
  • Periodic Financial Reporting

8. Maintenance & AMC Management

Maximize asset uptime with a robust maintenance system. Track repair requests, manage Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), and keep detailed technician logs for historical analysis.

  • Maintenance History Logs
  • Technician Assignment
  • Repair Cost Tracking

9. Controlled Asset Disposal

Safely retire assets through a secure approval workflow. Automatically process inventory reductions, book value adjustments, and scrap generation upon administrator approval.

  • Secure Approval Gate
  • Automatic Scrap Integration
  • Asset Gain/Loss Tracking

10. Advanced Reporting & Analytics

Make data-driven decisions with comprehensive reporting. Generate detailed asset lists, movement history, maintenance expense reports, and depreciation schedules in PDF or Excel formats.

  • Customized PDF Reports
  • Movement Tracking Analytics
  • Export to Excel

⚙️ Installation

📁 Step 1: Copy Module

Copy the asset_management folder into your Odoo custom addons path.

/opt/odoo/custom/addons/asset_management
⚙️ Step 2: Install App

Activate Developer Mode → Go to Apps → Update Apps List → Search for Asset Management and Install.

🚀 Step 3: Restart Odoo

Restart your Odoo service for changes to take effect.

sudo systemctl restart odoo
✅ Step 4: Configure Asset Types

After installation, go to Assets → Configuration → Asset Types to set up your asset categories with depreciation rules, then start creating your first assets.
